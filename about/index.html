About the business

Helping meaningful work earn clearer recognition.

Popoola Toluwani Academic & Professional Services supports researchers, academics, STEM professionals and technology experts in strengthening their academic contributions, professional profiles and online presence.

What we focus on

Our work brings together academic writing, research publication support, professional profile development and digital visibility. We also help developers and technology founders improve how their GitHub repositories are presented and discovered.

How we work

  • Start from your goals and current materials
  • Agree a practical scope before work begins
  • Focus on accurate, authentic presentation
  • Keep your achievements and contributions at the centre
For immigration-related work, our service is profile development support only. We do not give legal advice, represent clients, or promise petition approval or other outcomes.
