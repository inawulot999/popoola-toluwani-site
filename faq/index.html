Frequently asked questions

A few useful things to know.

Who do you work with?

We support STEM professionals, researchers, academics, software engineers, AI/ML developers and technology founders who want to strengthen their professional recognition, academic profiles or online visibility.

Where are your services available?

We work remotely, with a primary focus on the United States, followed by the United Kingdom, Canada, Germany, Australia and India. Other locations can be discussed.

How much do services cost?

We don’t publish a fixed price list at this time. Contact us with your goals and we’ll discuss the scope and provide a quote.

Do you provide immigration legal advice?

No. Our support covers professional profile development and related academic or professional materials. We do not provide legal advice, represent applicants or guarantee immigration outcomes. For legal guidance, consult a qualified immigration attorney or accredited representative.

Can you guarantee publication, recognition, GitHub growth or immigration approval?

No. Outcomes depend on many factors outside our control. We focus on careful preparation, clear presentation and authentic visibility practices.

Do you use artificial GitHub engagement?

No. GitHub visibility support focuses on clear repository presentation and genuine community exposure, not fake stars, forks, watches or commits.

How do I get started?

Use the contact page to email us or send a WhatsApp message. Briefly describe your goal and the support you need; we’ll follow up to discuss next steps.
